Hannah Binney is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [MIT Laboratory for Nuclear Science](https://web.mit.edu/lns/index.html), where she tries to measure the neutrino mass with the [Project 8 Experiment](https://www.project8.org/).  Before that, she got her Ph.D. at the University of Washington working on the [Muon g-2 Experiment](https://muon-g-2.fnal.gov/).  She was interviewed about the experiment's exciting results for the [New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2023/08/10/science/physics-muons-g2-fermilab.html) and [Science Magazine](https://www.science.org/content/article/particle-mystery-deepens-physicists-confirm-muon-more-magnetic-predicted).
